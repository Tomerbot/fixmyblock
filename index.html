      About FixMyBlock

      See a broken streetlight or a pothole? Report it with a photo and a pin. When neighbors tap I saw it too, the problem climbs the Fix first list, so the city knows what matters most.

      This is a demo. Reports are saved only in this browser, on this device. The first reports on the map are sample data with drawn pictures, not real photos.
